My First Research Lesson: So What?

Questions I always keep in mind.

Questions I always keep in mind:

  1. Does your work change how people think about a problem, even if no product uses it yet?
  2. Could you name one specific audience and one specific belief or practice of theirs that could change because of your result?
  3. So what?
